About this site

A one-page site built only with semantic HTML5 elements and a small stylesheet. No frameworks, no JavaScript.

Elements used

ElementPurpose
<header>Introductory content and navigation
<nav>Major navigation links
<main>The dominant content of the page
<article>Self-contained composition
<section>Thematic grouping of content
<aside>Tangential content
<footer>Footer for its nearest section
What about <div>?

Use it when no semantic element fits. It carries no meaning on its own.
